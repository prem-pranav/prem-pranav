### 💼 Professional Overview

Seasoned engineering professional with **14+ years of experience** in the Banking and Financial Services industry, specializing in **Retail Banking**, **Payment Switches**, and **Cards & Payments**. Proven track record in the deployment, configuration, maintenance, and support of high-availability transaction processing environments.

- **Payment Switching:** Hands-on experience with the **BASE24-eps** switch on both **Tandem** and **Unix** platforms
- **Protocols & Standards:** Deep proficiency in **ISO8583** (1987 / 1993 messaging specifications) and **EMV** technology
- **Device Handlers & Auth:** Specialized in **ATM** / **POS** device handlers, transaction routing, and authorization interfaces
- **Security:** In-depth understanding of **HSM** (Hardware Security Modules) and transaction cryptographic protocols
- **Simulators & Testing:** Extensive validation across devices and simulators including **ASSET**, **VTS**, **MAS**, **MDS**, **FinSim** and **jPOS**
- **Software Engineering:** Proficient in **C++** and **Java** for core payment logic, alongside **Python** and **PHP**
